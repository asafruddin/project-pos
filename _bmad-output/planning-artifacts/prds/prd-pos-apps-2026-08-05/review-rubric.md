# PRD Quality Review — POS Apps — Phase 1 + Phase 2

## Overall verdict
This PRD holds up as a thesis-driven, honestly de-scoped Phase 1+2 contract: Instant Checkout and Offline Mode stay locked, operations land in waves, and the counter-metrics (SM-C1–C5) actually defend the bet instead of decorating it. Phase 1 FR-1–FR-32 remain a model of testable consequences; Phase 2 mostly copies that discipline. What is at risk at launch-caliber — real stock, real money, real Shifts — is not vision-fuzz or persona theater. It is money-path done-ness (whether a Shift gates selling, how Expected Cash treats Voids, how Return/Exchange/Refund finish at the counter) plus a Fast-path assumption pile that still includes hardware, tax, and tender shape. A decision-maker can fund 2A with confidence; green-lighting 2B Returns and 2C Shift as live-cash work still requires closing those gaps.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Trade-offs are named as choices, not smoothed into “balance.” The addendum’s options table records what was given up (coffee-shop UJs kept vs retail reframe; Day Close coexists with Shift rather than being replaced; oversell warns rather than hard-blocks; Loyalty redeem stays online-only). Guardrails (§9) and Non-Goals (§5) give a sponsor something to push against: “Do not treat Phase 2 success as SaaS tenant count,” “Do not require CRDT to close Phase 2.” Open Questions in §10 are actually open — tablet/printer, tax inclusive/exclusive, shared vs device-local hold, Store Credit expiry, Store #2 timing, cash-drawer hardware, split-tender methods — not rhetorical setups with the answer in the next sentence.

The weakness is that several of those opens are already consumed by in-scope money FRs, while the live-money vs demo bar is split across documents without a `[NOTE FOR PM]`. Vision §1 says “A pilot may put real money through the sell loop” and Phase 1 “is **not** judged by subscription growth”; the addendum overrides a “1-week live pilot preference gate” with “Phase 1 demo/portfolio gates (SM-1–3).” That split is coherent if you read both files. It is not decision-ready for Andi’s go/no-go: nothing in the PRD tells him whether 2C Shift (SM-9) is allowed to ship against a “Phase 1 Receipt-only demo” hardware matrix (Open Question 6). Zero `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts appear anywhere; remaining tensions were dumped into `[ASSUMPTION]` and §10 instead of being flagged as decisions still on the sponsor.

### Findings
- **high** Production envelope still open while 2C money FRs ship (§10 Q1, Q6, Q7; §4.13 FR-79; §4.18 FR-110; SM-9) — Open Question 6 asks “Hardware matrix for cash drawer (Shift count) vs Phase 1 Receipt-only demo?” while FR-79 already requires the cashier to “count the drawer” and SM-9 validates “Shift Expected Cash vs counted drawer.” Open Question 7 asks which split-tender methods exist in 2C “without a card gateway” while FR-110 already specifies split across methods. A decision-maker can approve the Shift epic without knowing whether a drawer or a second tender exists. *Fix:* Close Q6/Q7 (or tag an explicit `[ASSUMPTION]` per wave: 2C cash-only + on-screen count, drawer optional) before 2C is treated as build-ready.
- **medium** Dual demo / live-money bar with no PM note (§1 Vision; addendum “1-week live pilot”; SM-1–SM-5 vs SM-6–SM-9) — Phase 1 success is demo/portfolio; Phase 2 is “Run the business” with Stock, Refunds, and Shifts. The PRD never states which bar Andi uses to put real cash in the drawer. *Fix:* Add `[NOTE FOR PM]` that Phase 1 SM-1–3 do not authorize live-cash operation; live money requires SM-9 + a closed hardware envelope, or explicitly accept cash-in-a-box without a drawer.
- **medium** Tax Open Question is already an input to a report FR (§10 Q2; FR-97) — Q2 has no interim default; FR-97 says “tax uses Phase 1 open question default until set.” There is no default. *Fix:* State an `[ASSUMPTION]` (e.g. tax-inclusive display, tax line omitted until configured) or remove tax from FR-97 consequences until Q2 closes.

## Substance over theater — strong
Jobs in §2.1 earn their keep. Dewi’s Instant Checkout / Offline / Shift / Return path drives FR-6–FR-21, FR-62–FR-69, FR-75–FR-81. Sari’s approval job is a real Permission split (FR-53, FR-58, FR-67, FR-101), not a “values the product” sentence. Andi is explicitly *not* the daily PO approver — a decision, not a persona card. The Builder/demo presenter would be furniture in a pure ops PRD; here it is load-bearing for SM-5 and the locked Phase 1 bar.

Vision cannot be swapped into a generic POS: “A coffee-shop POS that can be demoed and shown in a portfolio: Instant Checkout and Offline Mode for a **single shop**” plus “A notebook wins if the POS dies when the router does.” Differentiation is the isolation rule (Media Provider “**must never sit on checkout, payment, Receipt, or Sync**”), not a claimed-novelty paragraph. NFRs in §8 are product-specific (quantity truth across Sync, two-surface discipline, media isolation, centralized Promotion/Loyalty rules) rather than “must be scalable / secure / reliable.” Security is one thin line, but it is not boilerplate theater.

No findings. Supervisor as a seeded role (FR-99/FR-101) is justified by testable cannots, not by an unused persona section.

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis is stated and used: sell reliably first (including when the network dies), then run the business on Dashboard without rewriting the sell path and without becoming an ERP. Feature order follows that thesis, not “what’s easy.” Guardrail §9: “Do not ship 2D Promotions/RBAC/multi-Store as a prerequisite for 2A Stock Ledger.” Waves in §6 match: 2A trusts Stock, 2B moves goods and money back, 2C wraps people and the drawer, 2D grows. Phase 1 FR-1–FR-32 stay locked; Phase 2 “must not break these FRs” (§4.2).

Success Metrics validate the thesis rather than counting activity. SM-1–SM-3 are journey completions; SM-6–SM-10 are operational truth (ledger, receiving, Refund Permission, Shift difference, media isolation). Counter-metrics are doing real work: SM-C1 (MRR must not gate Phase 1), SM-C2 (feature breadth delaying Instant Checkout), SM-C3 (ERP completeness), SM-C4 (checkout latency regressions), SM-C5 (Media Provider uptime as a checkout dependency). MVP scope kind is coherent: Phase 1 is problem-solving (the Sale still happens); Phase 2 is an operations platform on the same sell path. This is not a backlog with headings.

No findings.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Phase 1 is unforgiving in the right way. FR-10–FR-12 make Sale completion a testable pair (payment **and** Receipt success; print failure ⇒ incomplete ⇒ no Stock). FR-15–FR-20 separate Sale success from Sync pending. FR-24 specifies the Day Close gate: finish disabled until Sync drains “**or** cashier explicitly acknowledges remaining unsynced Sales (recorded).” Almost every FR has a verifiable consequence; “clear error” appears (FR-3, FR-14, FR-42, FR-89) but is backed by deny/proceed/block behavior, not left as an adjective.

Phase 2 copies the pattern for catalog, ledger, receiving, and media isolation (FR-41’s “Network capture of Instant Checkout shows no Media Provider request” is an exemplary done-condition). It breaks down on cash and settlement — the slices that make this launch-caliber. An engineer can implement FR-75–FR-81 as written and still leave Instant Checkout ungated by Shift, Expected Cash deaf to Voids, and UJ-7’s customer waiting in an unspecified handoff. Those are not vague adjectives; they are missing consequences on FRs that otherwise look done.

### Findings
- **high** Open Shift does not gate Instant Checkout (UJ-8; FR-6–FR-13; FR-75; FR-111) — UJ-8’s path is “Open Shift (opening cash) → sell / Cash In / Cash Out / Refunds → close Shift.” FR-75 only records opening cash and rejects a second open “until close.” FR-111 blocks *Day Close* while a Shift is open, not *selling* while a Shift is closed. Phase 1 FR-6–FR-13 gain no Shift precondition. After 2C ships, Dewi can still complete SM-1 with no open Shift; SM-9 then measures a drawer that did not own those Sales. *Fix:* Add a testable consequence: either “Checkout disabled unless Shift status is open/active (2C+)” or an explicit `[ASSUMPTION]` that Shift is optional overlay and Sales with `shift_id = null` are in-scope (and SM-9 excludes them).
- **high** Expected Cash omits Void (FR-78; FR-76; FR-63) — Formula is “opening cash + cash Sales + Cash In − Cash Out − cash Refunds.” FR-76 attaches “complete Sales, Cash In, Cash Out, and cash Refunds” to the Shift. FR-63 Voids a complete Sale and reverses Stock; it does not reverse Expected Cash or un-attach the Sale. A same-day cash Void leaves the drawer math wrong by design. *Fix:* Define Void’s cash effect (decrease Expected Cash / reverse the cash-Sale line on the Shift) and add a consequence: Void of a cash Sale changes Expected Cash by the tendered cash amount.
- **high** Return / Exchange / Refund settlement is not done-complete (UJ-7; FR-67; FR-68) — UJ-7 climax is “Customer is made whole.” FR-67 says “Cashier may complete inventory decision and then wait for Refund” with no session rule (Sari PIN at Register vs Dashboard later; customer still at the counter vs come-back; whether the Return can be parked). FR-68’s consequences are only “Exchange does not require a second unrelated Sale id” and “Store Credit without a Customer is rejected” — no even-exchange vs price-difference, no tender of the difference, no Stock movements for the replacement leg, no statement whether FR-67’s Refund Permission applies to the money leg of an Exchange. *Fix:* Specify the wait/approve path (manager PIN in-session **or** parked Return, customer not blocked on Instant Checkout) and Exchange money rules (even / collect / refund difference) with ledger + Shift consequences.
- **medium** Oversell “warn only” has no actor, surface, or moment (FR-50) — “no hard stop on oversell in Phase 2; warn only” so Instant Checkout “never waits on a live count.” Consequence: “Oversell does not block Checkout; negative or zero is visible after Sync.” Warn *whom*, *where*, *when*? Cashier on add-to-cart, cashier after Sync, or only Dashboard low-Stock (FR-50’s list)? *Fix:* Name the warn surface (e.g. Dashboard only after Sync; Cashier never blocks and never waits) so SM-4 cannot be failed by a live qty round-trip.
- **medium** Void “same calendar day” is unbound (FR-63; UJ-7 edge) — “same calendar day” has no timezone, Store-local vs device clock, or relationship to Day Close. After Day Close, is yesterday’s Sale a Return-only path (FR-64) even if it is still “today” in UTC? *Fix:* Define Void window as Store-local calendar date **and** before Day Close for that Register (or whatever rule you choose), with a test: post–Day Close same-calendar-date Sale cannot Void.
- **medium** Promotion types are a laundry list with two consequences (FR-87) — “percentage, fixed, buy-X-get-Y, bundle, product/category, customer-group, minimum purchase, happy hour, Coupon, Voucher.” Testable consequences: inactive does not apply; date/time window is honored. BXGY, stacking vs FR-89’s “one Coupon,” and happy-hour clock (device vs Store) are untestable. Acceptable only if 2D treats a subset as the gate. *Fix:* Name the 2D done-subset (e.g. percent, fixed, one Coupon, optional happy hour) and move the rest to Non-Goals or a later wave.
- **low** Security NFR has no PIN/password bounds (§8) — “Passwords and POS PIN not logged in plaintext” is the entire security bar besides FR-32/FR-100. No lockout, retry limit, or PIN-at-rest rule. *Fix:* One consequence: failed POS PIN retries are bounded and do not log the PIN; lockout behavior named.

## Scope honesty — adequate
Omissions do real work. §5 is not a ritual: SaaS/MRR, KDS, warehouse app, live card gateway, CRDT, GL, recipe depletion, and “Becoming an ERP” are out, with feature-level Out of Scope under 4.1, 4.3, and 4.5 so they cannot silently crawl back into locked FRs. Waves in §6 are delivery order, not a hidden cut. The addendum’s “Intentional overrides of `docs/01-business/`” table is the most honest artifact in the set — it shows where this PRD *disagrees* with source docs and why.

Fast-path tagging is consistent: dozens of `[ASSUMPTION]` markers, indexed in §11, labeled “Phase 2 (Fast path — confirm on review).” That is the right mechanic. At launch-caliber it is also the problem. Open-item density is ~7 Open Questions + ~30 Phase 2 index lines + 0 `[NOTE FOR PM]` on a PRD that already includes P0+P1 and live Refunds/Shifts. The rubric’s warning applies: high counts on a green-light-to-build PRD are a blocker for the money waves, even though they are acceptable for 2A catalog/ledger. Honesty is strong; readiness of the open-item pile is not.

### Findings
- **high** Fast-path assumption density vs launch-caliber stakes (§11 Phase 2; §10; zero `[NOTE FOR PM]`) — Phase 2 index alone runs from Media Provider vendor through oversell, Void approval, Loyalty, Promotion fail-open, COGS method, RBAC mapping, and split tender. Several are load-bearing for real cash (Shift close with difference, cashier Void needs manager, Refund manager-only, hold device-local). Tagged is better than silent; unconfirmed is not build-ready for 2B/2C. *Fix:* Promote money-path assumptions (Shift gate, Void→cash, Refund handoff, tender methods, drawer hardware) to decided FRs or `[NOTE FOR PM]` with owner + revisit-before-wave; leave catalog/media assumptions tagged.
- **medium** “Resolved this finalize” is still an assumption (§10 last paragraph; FR-58) — “Purchase Orders, Stock Opname, and Stock Transfers are approved by Store Manager or Admin. Owner is go/no-go… `[ASSUMPTION]`.” Resolved and assumed cannot both be true. *Fix:* Drop the tag if Andi confirmed; keep the tag and move it out of “Resolved” if he did not.
- **low** `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` tag unused (§5, §6.6) — Equivalent prose exists (“Out of this PRD entirely,” feature Out of Scope). Downstream grep for the canonical tag will miss them. *Fix:* Optional — add the tag on the three easiest-to-misread cuts (count-on-POS, offline Dashboard receiving, live card).

## Downstream usability — adequate
This is chain-top (existing Phase 1 UX + architecture; this update feeds Phase 2 UX, architecture, and stories). The Glossary is load-bearing and mostly stable: Sale vs Receipt vs Sync, Day Close vs Shift, Void vs Return vs Refund, Stock Ledger vs Stock Adjustment vs Stock Opname. UJ-1–UJ-9 each name a protagonist inline (Dewi, Andi, Budi, Sari as approver). FR IDs are unique and contiguous FR-1–FR-112. SM IDs are unique (SM-1–SM-14, SM-C1–SM-C5). §6.4 lists FR-110, FR-111, FR-112 so wave extraction can still find them.

Extract-by-section will not. FR-111 lives in §4.4 with an honest note (“ID continues the global sequence”). FR-110 (split tender) and FR-112 (Customer-specific price) are 2C cashier Checkout requirements parked at the bottom of §4.18 Multi-Store. Register is a 2C Shift key (`[ASSUMPTION: one open Shift per Register]`) but Stores and Registers are FR’d in 2D (FR-104). Cloudinary leaks into Vision and SM-C5 despite the addendum’s vendor rule. Those are the extractability failures.

### Findings
- **medium** 2C Checkout FRs live under Multi-Store (§4.18 FR-110, FR-112; §4.4 FR-111; §6.4) — FR-110: “Cashier may split payment across methods… *(Phase 2C cashier checkout)*.” FR-112: “Customer-specific selling price *(Phase 2C)*.” A story pass over §4.12–§4.13 will miss tender and price override. *Fix:* Move FR-110/FR-112 under Instant Checkout or Customers/Shift (2C), leave ID numbers stable, and keep §6.4 as the wave index.
- **medium** Register is used in 2C and defined in 2D (FR-75; FR-104; Glossary “Register”) — Shift uniqueness is “one open Shift per Register” in 2C; “Admin can define Stores and Registers” is FR-104 in 2D. Phase 1 has “exactly one Store record (even if the UI never showed it)” but no matching implicit Register for 2C. *Fix:* State the 2C stub: one implicit Register per deployment until FR-104, and FR-75 uniqueness applies to that stub.
- **low** Vendor name in the PRD spine (§1; SM-C5; Glossary vs addendum) — Vision: “Cloudinary (the named Media Provider) enriches the catalog.” SM-C5: “any design that makes SM-1 require Cloudinary fails this counter-metric.” Addendum: “PRD capability = Product Media / Media Provider; Cloudinary named here.” *Fix:* Keep SM-C5 as Media Provider; leave Cloudinary in addendum and the Glossary assumption.

## Shape fit — strong
The product is multi-stakeholder B2B with a meaningful cashier UX and a second operations surface — the shape the rubric says should carry named-protagonist UJs. It does: UJ-1–UJ-3 locked coffee-shop sell path; UJ-4–UJ-9 for media, opname, receiving, Return, Shift, Loyalty attach. Capability-spec FRs sit where journeys would be overhead (RBAC matrix, report tiles). Brownfield discipline is explicit: Phase 1 journeys “preserved,” FR-1–FR-32 locked, existing UX/architecture “cover Phase 1; this update does not duplicate them.” Chain-top traceability is attempted (Glossary, IDs, wave lists).

The tail of 2D (Promotions, Stock Transfer, multi-Store) is more capability list than journey. That matches P1 growth, not a consumer checkout redesign. Not over-formalized for a two-surface POS; not under-formalized on the sell path.

### Findings
- **medium** 2D growth has SMs without protagonists (SM-12–SM-14; no UJ for Promotions, RBAC, or Stock Transfer) — Chain-top UX for “same Cart Panel totals the same discount in both surfaces” (FR-88) and “Draft → … → Received” transfers (FR-107) will author journeys from FRs. Fine for P1 if intentional. *Fix:* One short UJ for Sari configuring a happy-hour Promotion that Dewi sees at Checkout, and one for Budi receiving a Stock Transfer — or a sentence in §2.3 that 2D is capability-spec on purpose.
