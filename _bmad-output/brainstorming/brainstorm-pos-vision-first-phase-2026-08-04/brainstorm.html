TECHNIQUE 02

Job to Be Done

Vision lists users but not jobs. Phase 1 should be a JTBD matrix, not an app list. Modern POS wins if the cashier job is 10× better than Excel + calculator — not if the monorepo is elegant.

Cashier Close the sale. Survive the rush.
Close the sale fast without thinking — not browse dashboards
Recover from mistakes (wrong item / qty) without manager shame
Keep selling when Wi‑Fi dies during lunch rush
Prove cash drawer matches sales at end of day
Store Manager Trust the floor. Restock with eyes open.
Know what sold / what is low before the restocking trip
Trust that cashiers cannot silently void everything
Owner Money in. Money out. Then grow.
See money in / money out without opening an ERP
Open a second branch without rewriting the system
Others Accountant · Admin · Customer · Warehouse
Accountant: export clean daily sales for tax / bookkeeping
Admin: onboard a new cashier in minutes, not a training week
Customer (indirect): get out of line quickly with correct price
Warehouse (later): pick / receive without touching cashier UI

Hire for speed-to-sale

Big search, recent items, barcode, hotkeys — not pretty animations.

Hire for trust

Immutable sale log + supervised voids beats fancy analytics.

Hire for resilience

Offline queue of sales > cloud-only perfect inventory.

Hire for clarity

One tax rule that is always correct > many half-broken tax engines.

Phase 1 JTBD pack — the jobs that must clear the bar:

Sell Take payment Print / show receipt Adjust stock by 1 Day close

Missing JTBD in vision.md

  • Hardware reality (printers, drawers, scanners, tablets) as first-class
  • Training / onboarding path for non-technical cashiers
  • Dispute / refund trust model (who can reverse what)
  • End-of-day cash reconciliation ritual
  • Price changes mid-day without breaking open carts
  • Barcode conflicts / PLU / weighed items (retail vs F&B)
  • Multi-payment split tender (cash + card) in one sale
  • Park / hold sale when customer forgets wallet

If owner JTBD is SaaS multi-branch, phase 1 should prove one store first with a migration path. If F&B modifiers / KDS are in future apps, decide now whether retail-only phase 1 is intentional.

Insight Vision is strong on architecture jobs; thin on cashier / manager daily jobs that define MVP.

CLIMAX

Synthesis

Is vision.md enough for modern POS first phase?
Enough as north star.
Not enough as phase-1 scope.

Vision is a strong destination — offline, performance, modular monorepo, non-ERP. The phase 1 gap: no IN/OUT cut, thin JTBD for cashier / manager, no hardware spike, and platform breadth before the wedge.
